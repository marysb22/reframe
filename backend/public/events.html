We're proud to share our accreditation!

Reframe has been officially accredited by the Lebanese Order of Psychologists (LOPsy) as a Training Center in Systemic Psychotherapy, from October 1, 2025 to October 31, 2027.

This accreditation reflects our ongoing commitment to excellence, integrity, and evidence-based practice in the field of mental health, and it reinforces our mission to empower professionals across the Middle East through high-quality, culturally relevant training and supervision.

We extend our heartfelt thanks to our team, supervisors, and community for being part of this journey. Together, we're shaping a future where mental health education and practice are accessible, ethical, and deeply human.

Training & Professional Development

Structured learning opportunities designed to support growth, skill-building, and ethical clinical practice across different career stages.

Short-term trainings are concise sessions designed to introduce new ideas, tools, or developments in mental health practice.

Format:

  • Online
  • Duration: 2–4 hours

Focus areas include:

  • Updates on therapeutic techniques
  • Introduction to theories and models
  • Presentation of research findings and clinical insights
  • Sharing practice-based experiences and intervention outcomes

Best suited for: Professionals seeking focused learning and timely updates.

Mid-term trainings provide structured and immersive learning experiences aimed at developing and strengthening professional skills.

Format:

  • In-person or online
  • Duration: Half day to two weeks

Focus areas include:

  • Applied clinical skills
  • Practice-oriented learning
  • Guided exercises and case discussions

Best suited for: Practitioners seeking hands-on learning and skill enhancement.

Long-term trainings are designed to support deep and sustained learning through an integrated structure.

Core components:

  • Theoretical training
  • Ongoing supervision

Focus areas include:

  • Advanced conceptual understanding
  • Clinical application and case formulation
  • Progressive competency development

Best suited for: Professionals seeking structured, long-term clinical training.

Supervision services focus on supporting ethical, reflective, and effective clinical practice.

Format:

  • Individual or group supervision
  • Online or in-person

Focus areas include:

  • Case discussion and formulation
  • Clinical reflection
  • Professional development and ethical practice

Best suited for: Practicing psychotherapists seeking supervision and clinical support.
