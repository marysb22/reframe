Create a new group

Creates a complete group in one step -- 1 Master Trainer, any number of Trainers (ToT), and their Trainees. IDs auto-generate unless you set them. The Master Trainer and any Trainers share one password; every Trainee in the group shares another. To add more Trainers to an existing group later, use Create Trainer or open the group under All Groups.
